The other number formats are NUMERIC-family Issuer formats. They are placed in ADHOC (`0x0001`) format numbers `0x0700`-`0x0704`.

- They include complex numbers (COMPLEX64, COMPLEX128), a normalized fraction (RATIONAL-I64), a binary64 closed interval, and a binary32 quaternion.
